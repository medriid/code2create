## Reading the field

The participant portal's own example ideas are unusually technical:
post-pull-request collaboration, subagent tracing, heterogeneous distributed
inference, offline UPI, universal sync, Nix-based reproducibility, WebTransport
abstractions, a minimal mTLS mesh, cross-protocol DeFi risk, JSX-like interfaces
in Rust or Go.

That is a warning, not a shopping list. It says shallow dashboards will not
place. It does **not** say to imitate those specific ideas — several of them are
research projects wearing a hackathon costume.

FILAMENT OS matches that ambition through distributed coordination and failure
handling, while staying inside what five people can actually explain under
questioning. That second half is the part teams get wrong.
